Teaching & outreach

I have taught undergraduate physics laboratories at UMass Dartmouth since 2023, and designed and taught astronomy curricula for several hundred students before that. Explaining how we detect gravitational waves to someone who has never heard of them is a large part of why I enjoy this work.
