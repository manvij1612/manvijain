Research experience

  • Doctoral Researcher, Caudill Gravitational-Wave Group

    2025–

    University of Massachusetts Dartmouth.

    • Constructing matched-filter template banks that incorporate tidal effects for binary neutron star systems; running injection campaigns to characterise template-bank recovery toward a future BNS search.
    • Derived analytic waveform equations in Mathematica for precessing compact binaries in dynamical Chern–Simons gravity; Fisher-matrix parameter-estimation analysis in progress.

  • Graduate Research Assistant

    2024–2025

    UMass Dartmouth / LIGO–Virgo–KAGRA Collaboration.

    • Ran precessing binary black hole searches on LIGO O1 and O2 data with the GstLAL pipeline.
    • Packaged a precessing-harmonics waveform-generation code into a reusable module for pipeline use.
    • Quantified how weak detector glitches injected onto simulated signals bias signal recovery.
    • Contributed the precessing-search analysis underlying Schmidt et al., Phys. Rev. Lett. 133, 201401 (2024).
